---
name: unpack
description: Re-explain a step the user got lost on, with the context that was assumed. Use when the user says "/unpack", "wait, what", "I don't follow", "where did that come from", "how did you get there", or questions a jump in your reasoning.
---

The user lost the thread at some specific point. Find that point and fill in what
you skipped over.

Say what you assumed they already knew. That assumption is almost always the
actual gap, not the conclusion itself.

Then walk the step again from the last thing they clearly did follow. Do not
restart from the beginning; that buries the gap in material they already have.

If you skipped an inference, name it as an inference and say what it rests on. If
you skipped a fact about their setup, state the fact and where it comes from
(a file, a command you ran, a measurement).

If on re-reading you find the step was actually wrong, say so plainly and correct
it. A question about your reasoning is sometimes a correct objection.
