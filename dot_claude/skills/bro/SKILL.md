Restate your previous message in plain human language.

Zero jargon. If a technical term is unavoidable, define it in the same breath.

Do not add new information, do not re-research, and do not defend the original
wording. This is a translation of what you already said, not a second attempt at
the problem.

Lead with the single thing that actually matters. If the original had one real
conclusion buried under supporting detail, that conclusion is now the first
sentence.

Drop every header, bullet, table and bold run unless the content is genuinely a
sequence or a comparison. Most of the time this comes out as two to five sentences
of prose.
